## What the file contains

One sheet, `Product Statistics`, 5 data rows. Aggregate UPI volume and value by month for
FY 2026-27 to date.

| Column | Contents |
|---|---|
| `Month` | Month-year label |
| `Volume (In Mn.)` | Total UPI transactions, millions |
| `Avg. Daily Volume (In Mn.)` | Daily mean |
| `Value (In Cr.)` | Total value, crore INR |
| `Avg. Daily Value (In Cr.)` | Daily mean |

**August-2026 is a partial month.** Volume divided by average daily volume gives 19.0 days,
not 31. Any month-over-month comparison including August compares 19 days against 30 or 31.
The file does not label it partial; the arithmetic is what reveals it.

## What it does not contain

This is **all UPI**, not Autopay and not mandates. There is no split by product, no
success/failure counts, no failure reasons, and nothing that separates recurring debits
from one-time payments. It gives the denominator for a rate, never the numerator.
