## Figures present

NPCI's own switch reported **100% uptime and zero incidents** for July 2026.

Read alongside the July downtime workbook — which records 11 banks, 27 incidents and 46.7
hours of downtime in the same month — this says the observed outage was entirely
**issuer-side**. The central switch was up; individual member banks were not.

## What it does not contain

Only July 2026. There is no equivalent uptime file for April, May or June in `data/`, so
the "NPCI switch was up" observation is confirmed for one month of the four and assumed,
not evidenced, for the rest.
